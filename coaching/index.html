Not life coaching

Transformational coaching starts with who you are being.

Life coaching works on what you do. Transformational coaching works on who you are being, the qualities you express, and the beliefs underneath them.

The way you see the world shapes every thought, action and result. Lisa dives into your mindset and the hidden beliefs running it. When you see them, the shift is often immediate, and the results come in quantum leaps rather than small steps.

And even if you are a focused go-getter, you may be in your own way, operating on top of stops that keep you pushing instead of working with flow.

What coaching with Lisa does

Eight things you can count on.

  • Clarify and expand your heartfelt vision.
  • Identify, set and accomplish clear, measurable goals.
  • Define, or redefine, what is important to you.
  • See the hidden beliefs and interpretations that do not serve you.
  • Find the actions, and the qualities, it takes to produce the result.
  • Break through perceived barriers and go for the dream.
  • Stay accountable to your goals, through whatever gets in the way.
  • Think exponentially, and produce quantum leap results quickly.
