# Instagram DMs to people who don't follow Lisa yet

**Goal:** start real conversations with people who fit the course, move them to the scorecard, and invite the warmest. Expect 0 to 1 seats by Dec 14; the conversations and quiz leads pay off for the next cohort too.
**Code:** FRIEND200, link `https://www.lisakalmin.com/course/?code=FRIEND200&utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=mwm-dec-prospect`

## Who goes on the list (aim for 150 to 200)

Look for people who:

- Engage (comment, not just like) on accounts close to Lisa's world: money mindset coaches, abundance and law-of-attraction teachers, Hay House authors, women's business communities, and the podcasts Lisa has been a guest on.
- Are coaches, consultants, realtors, practitioners, creatives or small business owners (check the bio).
- Post about money stress, raising rates, pricing, "abundance," "worthiness," or growing their business.
- Are active in the last 2 weeks, with a real, personal account (skip brands and very large creators).

Sources: comments on posts from similar coaches, hashtags like #moneymindset #abundancemindset #womeninbusiness #coachesofinstagram #realtorlife, and attendees tagged at local Orange County women's business events.

Log each person in a sheet: handle, why they fit (one line), date of warm-up, date of DM 1, reply, quiz, invite, outcome.

## Pace and safety

- Warm up first. **Follow and leave 2 thoughtful comments over 2 days** before DMing. A DM from someone who has already engaged lands in their main inbox more often, and reads as human.
- **No more than 20 new prospect DMs a day**, mixed in with normal activity. Instagram limits accounts that send many identical messages to strangers.
- No links in DM 1 or DM 2. Change the first line for every person.

## Warm-up · Days 1 and 2

Comment on two of their recent posts. Real comments, more than four words, about what they actually said. Examples:

- "That line about pricing from your gut instead of your fear. Yes. 👏"
- "Love that you shared this. It takes courage to talk about money this openly."

---

## DM 1 · Day 3: Open on their content

> Hi {{name}}! I loved your post about {{specific thing}}. {{one genuine sentence about why}}. I work with people on their money story and that's such a common one. Can I ask what got you thinking about it?

If they don't post about money:

> Hi {{name}}! I've been enjoying your posts about {{their topic}}. Quick question as a fellow {{business owner / coach / creative}}: what's the hardest part of the money side of your work right now?

No reply in 3 days: one nudge, then stop.

> Totally understand if this got buried! Would genuinely love to hear your take. 😊

When yes:

> Here it is: https://www.lisakalmin.com/quiz/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm-prospect
> I'd love to hear your result!
