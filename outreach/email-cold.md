## Rules before sending anything

- **Never send from Kit or from lisakalmin.com.** Kit's terms ban purchased or scraped lists, and spam complaints would hurt delivery to the main list. Use a separate lookalike domain (for example `lisakalmincoaching.com`) with 2 to 3 inboxes in a cold-email tool (Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist).
- **Warm-up:** new inboxes need about 2 weeks of warm-up. Starting now, keep volume to **20 to 30 sends per inbox per day** and expect the first email to go out around Nov 30. This is why cold is a small channel for this launch.
- **US only.** Exclude Canada (CASL) and the EU/UK (GDPR).
- **CAN-SPAM:** a real postal address and a one-line opt-out in every email. Honor opt-outs within 10 days (the tool does it automatically).
- **Verify the list** (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce) and drop anything risky. Keep bounces under 2%.
- **Plain text only**, no images, no tracking pixels, one link at most per email. Every quiz link carries `utm_source=cold&utm_campaign=mwm-dec&utm_content=<segment>` so Kit can tag them `cold-quiz`.
- **Stop the sequence** on any reply. Lisa (or her assistant, as Lisa) answers replies by hand within a day.
