## How to work it

- **Start with the warmest 50**, then work outward. In order: past clients and course alumni, people who commented or replied to a story in the last 60 days, masterclass registrants who follow, people who regularly like posts.
- **Track every thread** in a simple sheet: handle, date of DM 1, replied (Y/N), quiz pattern, invited (Y/N), bought (Y/N).
- **About 30 to 40 new conversations a day** is a safe pace for Instagram. Replies to existing threads don't count against that.
- **Never paste the same text to everyone.** Use the scripts as a base, and change the first line for each person: their name, something they posted, how long they've followed.
- **Voice notes win.** A 20-second voice note from Lisa for DM 1 or the invite gets far more replies than text. If an assistant sends the texts, Lisa still records the voice notes.
- **No link in the first message.** Instagram suppresses link-first DMs, and it reads like a pitch.
