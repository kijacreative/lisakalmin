What Money Wealth and Miracles is

More than a course. A shift in how you see money.

Your financial freedom isn’t just a possibility. It is an inevitable reality. What it takes is a tangible shift in your perspective, and that is what a miracle is.

Money Wealth and Miracles is a live online coaching program built on Lisa’s book, The Problem Is How You See The Problem. Over six sessions you get the knowledge, the tools and the room to reshape your financial reality, with Lisa coaching you through it in real time.

    Questions

    Before you join.

    Where does the course happen?

    Online, live on Zoom. You join from wherever you are; all you need is a computer or phone with a camera and a quiet spot for 90 minutes.

    How much time does it take?

    Six live sessions of 90 minutes over about seven weeks, plus a weekly breakthrough assignment to put what you learn into practice.

    How big are the groups?

    Between 15 and 30 people. Small enough that Lisa coaches you directly in the live Q&A, large enough that you learn from other people’s breakthroughs too.

    Do I need to have read Lisa’s book?

    No. The course is built on The Problem Is How You See The Problem, and the book is a good companion, but you do not need it to start.
