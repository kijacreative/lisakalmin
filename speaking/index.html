Five tips to impact your audience.

We all speak in front of people, whether it is two or two thousand. We are always causing possibility and action, or we are not.

01

Know your purpose.

What experience are you committed to leaving your audience with? What difference will your time with them make? If all you want is a sale, get clear on the value you will give. The sale happens from there, and people can sense an agenda a mile away.

02

Clear yourself before.

Clear anything filling your head, a fight with your partner or the fear of forgetting a line, by breathing, meditating or talking it through with a mentor or coach. The more clear and available you are, the more space and impact you create.

03

Make it participatory.

We learn and are influenced by what we experience in our cells. Evoke participation, generate sharing and experiences, and your influence will skyrocket.

04

Stay out there, with them.

Nothing is happening in your head; everything is happening in the audience. If you go into your head to remember your talk, come right back out and let your intuitive knowing take over.

05

Talk about real life, with personal stories.

Make it real in their life experience. Personal, authentic stories, relevant to your topic or an analogy for it, connect you with your audience. Lisa uses snow skiing, leaning down the mountain, to show how much of life we get to surrender to instead of control.
