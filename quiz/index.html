Your Money Mindset Pattern · 30 to 99

Money Manifester

You are proactive in the face of your circumstances.

  • You see opportunity even in challenges.
  • You believe in your value.
  • You attract wealth and opportunities.
  • You create value for other people.

You know there is another level for you, but don’t know how to access it!

Your Money Mindset Pattern · 100 to 169

Money Manager

You sometimes overcome your obstacles and sometimes don’t.

  • You worry or stress about money even when you have it.
  • You are looking at how to “get” more money rather than seeing the flow of money as energetic.
  • You micromanage your finances, or the people who handle your finances.

You desire to feel confident and joyful about your money and finances!

Your Money Mindset Pattern · 170 to 234

Money Clueless

You work hard but feel disconnected from money.

  • You find yourself disappointed in your finances at the end of the month.
  • You feel ill-equipped to handle your finances.
  • You are too busy to care most of the time.

You know you are in the way of your financial security and want to change!

Your Money Mindset Pattern · 235 to 300

Money Victim

You feel like the system is rigged against you.

  • Every time you try something new it fails.
  • You feel helpless and powerless in regard to accumulating wealth.
  • You feel trapped in a financial loop.

You are looking for a way out of your scarcity and frustration!
